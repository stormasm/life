**All 12 of Cormac McCarthy’s novels** have been translated into Spanish. In addition, his major theatrical and screenplay works have also been published in Spanish.

| **Suttree** (1979) | *Suttree* |
| **Blood Meridian** (1985) | *Meridiano de sangre* |
| **All the Pretty Horses** (1992) | *Todos los hermosos caballos* |
| **The Crossing** (1994) | *En la frontera* |

### **Dramatic Works & Screenplays**

* **The Sunset Limited** (2006) – Published as *El Sunset Limited*  
* **The Counselor** (2013) – Published as *El consejero*

*Most of the official Spanish translations of McCarthy's body of work were translated by **Luis Murillo Fort** and published by **Literatura Random House / Mondadori**.*
