
Yash Santosh Thakur

May 2, 2004 - April 2, 2026

It is with profound grief and deep love that the family of Yash Thakur announces his passing on April 2, 2026. He passed peacefully and started his journey to the abode of his Creator. He was 21 years old, and in that brief time he filled our hearts with immense love.

Yash was born on May 2, 2004, in Weymouth, MA to Ashwini and Santosh Thakur. He spent his initial years in Pune, India and completed high school in Silver Spring, MD. He graduated from the University of Maryland with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science in May 2025. He worked for Amazon Web Services as a Software Development Engineer.

Yash was an exceptional young man whose intelligence, determination, and curiosity set him apart from an early age. A brilliant student, he consistently excelled throughout his academic journey, earning admiration from teachers and peers alike. His passion for learning and drive to succeed led him to graduate in just three years, an achievement that reflected both his dedication and remarkable ability.

Deeply passionate about technology, Yash had a strong interest in application development and worked with several programming languages. He was also an active contributor to open-source projects, generously sharing his knowledge and skills with the wider community, an embodiment of his collaborative spirit and desire to create meaningful impact.

Beyond his academic and professional accomplishments, Yash was a young man of many talents and quiet passions. He had a remarkable musical ear and could play instruments such as the tabla and piano with great ease and comfort, bringing joy to those who heard him. He also enjoyed staying active and engaged, with a love for tennis and a sharp mind for chess. In quieter moments, he found happiness in board games, puzzles, and reading pastimes that reflected both his intellect and thoughtful nature.

A man of few words, Yash possessed a subtle, witty sense of humor that delighted those who knew him well. His calm presence, gentle spirit, and understated charm left a lasting impression on everyone fortunate enough to know him.

He is deeply missed by his parents and everyone who knew him, who will carry his memory in their hearts always.

Though his time with us was far too brief, Yash’s impact was immeasurable. His legacy of brilliance, quiet strength, generosity, and warmth will never be forgotten.

In lieu of flowers, the family asks that donations be made to Rockville Help and/or Senior Connection of Montgomery County.

Services entrusted to Going Home Cremation and Funeral Care.
