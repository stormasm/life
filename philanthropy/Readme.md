### QCD

No, you cannot make a Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD) from an inherited IRA if you are under age 70½

Age Requirements

- Beneficiary Age: The person making (initiating) the distribution must be at least 70½ years old at the time the QCD is made.
- Owner's Age: The age of the original account owner at death—or your age when you inherited the account—does not matter.
- Under Age 70½: If you take a distribution from an inherited IRA before turning 70½, it counts as regular taxable income and cannot be treated as a tax-free QCD.
